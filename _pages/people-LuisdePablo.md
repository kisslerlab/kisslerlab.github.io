## Bio

I'm a PhD student in the Ecology and Evolutionary Biology department, and doing a concurrent MS in the Computer Science department at CU. Before moving to Colorado, I did my bachelor's in Biology and Music at Amherst College, with a certificate in Coastal and Marine Science. For as long as I can remember, I've been fascinated by nature and obsessed with trying to understand it. My current research focuses on identifying the hidden mechanisms that support ecological stability, using mathematical modeling, computer simulations, and publicly-available data.

Outside of work, I like to spend my time learning instruments, playing soccer, reading, and cooking.
